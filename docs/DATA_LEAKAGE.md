## 1. The Threat of Data Leakage in Cyclone Modeling

Tropical cyclone time-series datasets are notoriously susceptible to subtle data leakage that inflates evaluation metrics while destroying real-world operational generalization:
1. **Random Sample Shuffling:** Points from the same storm appear in both train and test sets, allowing models to memorize storm-specific idiosyncrasies.
2. **Global Preprocessing:** Computing standard scaler means or imputation medians across the entire dataset before splitting leaks test distribution information.
3. **Future Information in Features:** Incorporating measurements from $t > t_0$ (e.g. forward moving averages or post-analysis best-track smoothing) creates false predictive power.
4. **Target Leakage:** Accidental inclusion of future intensity or delta wind in the state vector.

## 2. 7-Point Leakage Prevention Controls

CycloneGuard implemented 7 strict structural firewalls in Sprint 5:

### 1. Storm-Wise Partition Firewalls
- Partitioning is performed strictly at the **storm identity** (`storm_id`) level.
- Unit tests enforce the mathematical assertion:
  ```python
  assert train_ids.isdisjoint(val_ids)
  assert train_ids.isdisjoint(test_ids)
  assert val_ids.isdisjoint(test_ids)
  ```
- **Audit Result:** PASSED. Train, Validation, and Test sets share 0 storm identifiers.

### 2. Preprocessing & Scaler Isolation
- The `CycloneFeatureScaler` is initialized and fitted **strictly on $X_{\text{train}}$**.
- Validation and test feature vectors are normalized using the stored training statistics ($\mu_{\text{train}}, \sigma_{\text{train}}$).
- `scaler.json` stores only training parameters.
- **Audit Result:** PASSED. Scaler fit never encounters validation or test rows.

### 3. Directional Temporal Causality
- The `CycloneState` at $t_0$ only ingests current observations ($t_0$) and historical observations ($t \le t_0$).
- Kinematics (motion speed, forward bearing) are derived strictly from $(t_0, t_0 - \Delta t)$.
- **Audit Result:** PASSED. Zero forward-looking time steps in feature extraction.

### 4. Target Isolation
- The target label $y = \text{is\_ri}_{24\text{h}}$ is generated by [`ml/data/sequences/ri_label.py`](file:///c:/Users/maaz2/OneDrive/Desktop/storm/ml/data/sequences/ri_label.py) as an external ground-truth variable.
- Target variables (`future_wind_kts`, `delta_wind_kts`, `is_ri`) are **never added to the feature registry or state encoder**.
- **Audit Result:** PASSED. Feature vectors contain 0 future target columns.

### 5. Training-Only Imputation Medians
- Missing feature fallback medians are calculated exclusively from $X_{\text{train}}$.
- Test instances with missing sensors are filled with training medians, accompanied by $x_{\text{is\_observed}} = 0.0$.
- **Audit Result:** PASSED. No test distributions leaked into imputers.

### 6. Strict Missing-Future Protocol
- Track points without a verified future observation within $[22\,\text{h}, 26\,\text{h}]$ (e.g. at dissipation or landfall) are assigned `status="UNAVAILABLE_MISSING_FUTURE"`.
- The pipeline rejects these points from supervised evaluation rather than interpolating synthetic labels.
- **Audit Result:** PASSED. Zero interpolated future labels.

### 7. Sequence Builder Retrospective Window
- The `SequenceBuilder` in [`ml/datasets/sequence_builder.py`](file:///c:/Users/maaz2/OneDrive/Desktop/storm/ml/datasets/sequence_builder.py) builds sequences looking strictly backward: $[t_{-12}, t_{-9}, t_{-6}, t_{-3}, t_0]$.
- **Audit Result:** PASSED. All sequence steps precede or equal $t_0$.
